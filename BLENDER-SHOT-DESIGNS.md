# Shot design before material

Earlier material-demo compositions are R&D ONLY and excluded. These combined shots require encoded sequence inspection; scripts and stills do not approve the moving image.

## A: GLASS × GIANT ARCHITECTURE × PARTICLES — glass-gate

Physical idea: FRAGILE / FLOATING — hard refractive enclosure with soft tissue and dry trapped inclusions.

1. Hero: An asymmetric fractured kilometre-scale glass 04 gate, with a hollow architectural silhouette, not a box.

2. Visual centre: The cyan split at the right-of-centre threshold; the white rim is secondary.

3. Depth: foreground: A partial near-black buttress cuts the left edge; refractive splinters pass the lens. midground: Fractured stepped decks, a metal spinal armature and the suspended lower gate. hero: Frosted glass arch, internal branching light and a single horizontal time fault. background: A distant 04 monument and three unequal relic fins in mist.

4. Camera: Low oblique approach → slip sideways past foreground → accelerate through the opened threshold.

5. Event: SPLIT / REVEAL / PASS. At 0.9s the judgement boundary lights; the upper arch freezes, lower arch separates; at 1.8s the camera enters the gap.

6. Entry: The previous numeric nave becomes a physically refractive architectural edge.

7. Exit: The opened glass threshold reveals distant architecture, then a hard cut flattens it into the following graphic geometry. No full-frame glass occlusion is claimed.

8. Neighbour connection: 19.851–22.55s between numeric nave and exploded geometry; retain number + horizontal cut DNA.

Palette: Cold smoke / near-black structure / cyan light seam. No rainbow.
Graphic DNA: 04 architecture, judgement boundary, asymmetric framing, impossible time fault

## B: WATER × JUDGEMENT LINE × REFLECTION WORLD — mirror-crossing

Physical idea: WET / HEAVY / INVERTED — a water film reveals a violet civilisation with its own gravity.

1. Hero: A thin water horizon separates an ivory impossible cantilever above from a violet aperture civilisation below.

2. Visual centre: The white horizon; then the rotating violet world visible inside the film.

3. Depth: foreground: A cropped submerged black shelf and refractive film edges. midground: Large fractured ivory cantilever overhead, broken dark terraces below. hero: Mirror horizon and the other world within it. background: Two continuous folded dark membranes enclose matte silver ribs in violet fog; a filled white aperture and far 08 silhouette establish the destination.

4. Camera: Skim at grazing angle → dive through the horizon → roll into the lower world → accelerate into the bright aperture.

5. Event: REFLECT / INVERT / ENTER. At 0.65s only the underwater world turns 90 degrees while the upper cantilever remains still; at 1.3s the camera penetrates the film.

6. Entry: The preceding magenta membrane flattens into a physical horizon.

7. Exit: White aperture fills the lens, matching the eight-frame white cut.

8. Neighbour connection: 44.7–46.5s between organic membrane and white graphic attack; lower world borrows the aperture language, not the upper reflection.

Palette: Ivory / ink surfaces; violet fog and magenta bounce light, white rim and exit. Saturation belongs to the air and light.
Graphic DNA: judgement horizon, different reflection world, 90-degree gravity fault, camera transition

## C: ORGANIC FIBERS × MECHANICAL APERTURE — fiber-aperture

Physical idea: TENSE / ORGANIC / MECHANICAL — bending nerves constrain a shutter, then release the camera into white light.

1. Hero: Five interlocking asymmetrical shutter blades inside a deep coherent fibre architecture.

2. Visual centre: A white aperture at the tunnel vanishing point, revealed as the blades peel apart.

3. Depth: foreground: Thick cropped metal fibres and one close cyan data strand. midground: Hundreds of tapering glass / metal fibres in organised helical depth bands. hero: Five-fold iris with a sharp, legible negative-space silhouette. background: A distant white aperture and hanging numeric cuts.

4. Camera: Off-axis inside traversal → shutter opening → fast pass into the far aperture.

5. Event: OPEN / PASS. The blades separate in 0.5s, fibres briefly contract, then camera passes the obstruction by 2.1s.

6. Entry: The preceding warm vault is compressed into a dark mechanical throat.

7. Exit: The aperture bleaches into the following scan attack.

8. Neighbour connection: 51.7–54.8s before the cyan scan and four-frame black boundary; organic weight + hard mechanical timing.

Palette: Near-black / silver glass / white aperture, rare cyan strands.
Graphic DNA: geometric iris, number cuts, sharp aperture mask, camera as transition

## D: ROCK / TERRAIN × NUMBER MONUMENT × SKY — relic-scrape

Physical idea: DRY / DUSTY / MASSIVE — eroded continent skin becomes a wipe while a numeral becomes inhabitable space.

1. Hero: The cracked surface of a floating continent forms the left foreground, framing a vast 08 monument beyond.

2. Visual centre: The sky gap and distant monument, with a cyan embedded fracture guiding the eye.

3. Depth: foreground: About half the frame is a rim-lit eroded relic slab with glass inlays. midground: Three unequal broken terraces and structural bridges inside the gap. hero: The continent edge and monumental 08 visible together. background: Blue-white sky, long distant beam and a second suspended landmass.

4. Camera: Scrape close to the relic surface, reveal the number through its gap, then let an edge sweep to complete occlusion.

5. Event: REVEAL / PASS / EXIT. The foreground crack widens, 08 is revealed, then a dark relic edge wipes the image to black.

6. Entry: Match the preceding tiny black seed to an extreme close dark mass.

7. Exit: Full-frame physical black occlusion, then the violet macro world begins.

8. Neighbour connection: 39.3–41s connecting seed threshold to tissue world; makes black a spatial transition, not just an overlay.

Palette: Blue-white distance / rough navy-black terrain / thin cyan inlay.
Graphic DNA: 08 civilisation, judgement sky line, extreme scale, physical occlusion

## E: PROCEDURAL LIFE × FINAL DAWN × MASSIVE SCALE — life-relic

Physical idea: LIVING / ROOTED / SUSPENDED — close leaves penetrate an enormous weightless inverted relic.

1. Hero: A tiny living fibre at the lens and a top-heavy suspended obelisk, split into three unequal inverted relic shards, share a thin reflective plane.

2. Visual centre: First a warm rim on one near-black living leaf; then the cyan internal fissure in the massive suspended relic.

3. Depth: foreground: A few large, close deep-green fibres; no grass lawn. midground: Fractured wet geometric platform with a few rooted fibres. hero: A hundreds-of-metres inverted obelisk with a jagged primary silhouette and two internal energy fissures. background: A second half-buried relic in cool haze and a thin judgement-line horizon.

4. Camera: Centimetres above the water film; pass the close leaf, rise and reveal the gigantic threshold.

5. Event: REVEAL / SPLIT. A leaf uncovers the relic; a cyan fissure expands while the horizon remains impossibly level.

6. Entry: The island canopy resolves into a macro living edge.

7. Exit: The obelisk edge becomes the next numeric monument.

8. Neighbour connection: 12–14.591s between archipelago and macro leaf; revisited only as a small surviving sprout after final black at 135s.

Palette: Cool dawn / near-black-green life / warm rims / one cyan seam.
Graphic DNA: judgement horizon, impossible architecture, life versus scale, graphic negative space
